LUMA DRIFT: OCEAN RUSH

Keep your
light moving.

A free, offline ocean game for iPhone and iPad. Guide a luminous manta, follow the gold lights, and weave through an endless ocean.

How to play

Drag left or right to steer. Collect lights and avoid obstacles. You have three lives. Challenges change every 100 metres, zigzag gates appear every 300 metres, and a faster underwater section begins every 2,000 metres.

Help and feedback

Email dardasar@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, game version, and what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

Progress and settings

Your best score and preferences are stored on your device. There is no account or cloud save. Deleting the app and its data removes local progress. Sound and visual quality can be changed in Settings.
