LUMA DRIFT

A little room
for privacy.

Luma Drift is an offline, single-player game. The game does not require an account and does not send your gameplay or personal information to a developer-operated server.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best score and preferences in local app or browser storage. These values are used only to remember your progress and settings on that device. They are not uploaded by the game.

No tracking

This version contains no advertising, analytics, tracking identifiers, social integrations, or in-app purchases. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

Removing local data

You can remove local game data by clearing this site's data in your browser, or by deleting the installed app and its data. Device backups are managed by your operating system and your own backup settings.

App Store and support

Apple may process information when you use the App Store or its platform services under Apple's own privacy policy. If you contact the publisher through the support details on the App Store listing, information you choose to send is handled separately from gameplay.

Publisher and support

Published by Daniel Balint. Contact dardasar@gmail.com for privacy or support questions. If you email us, we use the message and contact details you provide to respond and resolve your request. We retain correspondence only as long as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete it.

This support website

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical requests, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure it. We add no analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising. Gmail handles voluntary support correspondence. These services are separate from offline gameplay. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of information you have sent to support, object to its processing, or contact your data-protection authority. We process voluntary correspondence to answer your request and maintain the game. Apple handles its own platform services under Apple's privacy policy.

Changes

This policy describes the current version of Luma Drift. If a future version changes how the game handles information, this page will be updated.
