How do I build?

Choose a structure during preparation, then tap an empty tile inside the build zone. Tap an existing structure to upgrade or recycle it. Reposition Soldier moves your starting position without spending scrap.

How do I shoot?

Hold FIRE to attack the nearest enemy in range. Move with the left stick. PULSE damages nearby enemies and recharges in 12 seconds. Auto fire is available in Settings.

Where is my progress?

Progress saves on the device. Reinstalling or resetting local progress can remove your save. Cross-device cloud saves are not included.

Why are the online boards unavailable?

Game Center requires the native Apple app, an internet connection, and an eligible signed-in Game Center account. Offline gameplay remains available. The browser development preview only shows local records.

Purchases and refunds

Apple handles the App Store purchase. Use Apple's purchase-support process for billing or refund requests.

Privacy

The game contains no ads or tracking SDKs. See the privacy notice for local saves, optional Game Center, and voluntary support correspondence.
