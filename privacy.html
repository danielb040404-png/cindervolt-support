Cindervolt privacy policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026.

Publisher/controller: Daniel Balint
Privacy and support contact: dardasar@gmail.com
Public policy URL: https://danielb040404-png.github.io/cindervolt-support/privacy.html

This notice covers Cindervolt version 1.0 and its support website.

Local gameplay

Cindervolt stores your saved expedition, settings, earned cores, workshop upgrades, and personal records on your device. This information is used to continue your game and remember your preferences. The app does not send these local save files to a publisher-operated server. Your device backup settings may cause Apple to include this information in device backups. Cloud-save synchronization between devices is not included.

The game does not include advertising networks, analytics SDKs, tracking identifiers, or third-party account registration. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. Sound effects are generated on the device.

Optional Apple Game Center

You can play offline without signing in to Game Center. If you choose to connect through the leaderboards screen, the app uses Apple's GameKit service to authenticate your Game Center player and submit personal bests for expedition score, completed waves, enemies defeated, and largest outpost. Once connected, improved records may be submitted at wave completion and expedition end.

Apple processes Game Center account information and leaderboard records under its own privacy terms. Your Game Center alias and records may appear to other players according to Game Center's settings. The app retrieves and displays Game Center rankings within the game; the publisher does not operate a separate leaderboard database. Pending records are stored on your device and retried when you reconnect through the app or complete another wave while authenticated.

See Apple's privacy policy and your device's Game Center privacy settings for Apple's handling of account and service information, including storage locations, retention, and available controls.

Purchases

Apple handles the App Store purchase, payment information, receipts, and refund process. The game does not request or store payment-card details. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions in version 1.0. Apple may provide the publisher with App Store sales and reporting information under the publisher's Apple agreement; this is separate from the local game save.

Voluntary sharing and support

Selecting Share opens the device's sharing interface with a text field report containing your score, waves, and eliminations. It is sent only to the destination you choose. That destination handles the shared content under its own policies.

If you contact support, Daniel Balint receives the contact details, message, and attachments you choose to send through the support Gmail mailbox. Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, or other unnecessary sensitive data. Support information is used to answer your request and resolve problems, and retained only as long as needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the support address. Google processes email under its applicable service terms and privacy policy.

The public support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no publisher-added analytics, advertising, forms, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website, under GitHub's privacy statement. Visiting this website is separate from offline gameplay.

Choices, retention, and deletion

Local progress remains until you reset it in the game's settings, remove the app's data, or restore a different backup. Use Settings → Reset local progress to clear local saved progress and preferences. It does not remove records that have already been submitted to Game Center or copies in device backups.

Manage Game Center sign-in, profile visibility, and privacy through Apple's settings and privacy tools. Unsubmitted records remain locally queued unless you reset local progress. Resetting also clears that queue; a submission already in transit may still complete. Contact the publisher at the address above for questions about support correspondence or information the publisher actually holds. Apple controls requests relating to its own account and service data.

Where applicable, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. The response depends on the information held and applicable law. Voluntary support correspondence is processed to respond to your request and for the legitimate interest of maintaining and supporting the game; records required by law are retained to meet those obligations. Apple, Google, and GitHub describe their international processing and applicable safeguards in their own privacy notices linked above.

Children and content

The game contains stylized combat with fictional creatures. It is not presented as a child-directed service. The App Store age rating is determined from the final content questionnaire. The app does not solicit personal information from children. Apple controls eligibility and parental restrictions for its Game Center services.

Changes

The publisher will update this notice if the game's data practices change. Advertising, analytics, cloud saves, or other new online services are not part of version 1.0 and must not be added without reviewing privacy notices, disclosures, and any necessary consent. The effective date above will identify the current policy.
